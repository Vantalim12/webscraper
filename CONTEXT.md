# Direct Contract Outreach

Outreach to US-based organizations to pursue remote contract work from the Philippines.

## Language

**Contractor**:
A person seeking to provide development or automation work to an organization under a contract, rather than as its employee.
_Avoid_: Employee, job applicant

**Direct client**:
The organization that directly engages the contractor for paid work.
_Avoid_: Employer

**Contract prospect**:
A US-based organization being considered as a potential direct client. It need not have advertised an opening.
_Avoid_: Customer lead, employer

**Remote contract work**:
Contractor work performed from home in the Philippines for a US-based direct client.
_Avoid_: Employee position, work-from-home job

**Contact candidate**:
A publicly listed business email address collected for human review before outreach; collection alone does not establish that the address is suitable for a contract inquiry.
_Avoid_: Verified lead, qualified contact

**Approved contact**:
The single publicly listed business address selected for an agency after checking its purpose, source, and outreach history.
_Avoid_: Extracted address

**Manual outreach**:
A contract inquiry or follow-up that the user reviews and sends personally.
_Avoid_: Automated campaign

**Manual pilot**:
A two-week trial of personally researching and contacting prospective direct clients within a two-hour weekly outreach budget. It evaluates the outreach approach and where contact collection consumes effort.
_Avoid_: Scraper launch

**Interested reply**:
A response that opens a genuine discussion about paid contract work. Automatic responses and rejections are replies but are not interested replies.
_Avoid_: Any reply, signed contract

**Outreach sequence**:
An initial contract inquiry and at most one follow-up seven days later. Any reply, opt-out, or bounce ends the sequence.
_Avoid_: Drip campaign

**Suppressed contact**:
An address that must not receive outreach because it opted out or bounced. Its exclusion persists across new prospect lists and sending inboxes.
_Avoid_: Deleted lead

**Address-finding effort**:
Time spent locating published contact addresses for already-selected contract prospects. Company-fit research and message writing are separate activities.
_Avoid_: Prospect research

**Website-to-GHL integration**:
A connection that sends a prospective customer's website form submission into GoHighLevel.
_Avoid_: AI agent implementation
